Navigation Plan: Trees, Tabs, Back and Forward, Go To
=====================================================

Moving around a LogiCim project with WindowLib's parts from the HCP65 IDE: the Simulation and Subcircuits panes as
trees (CTreeContainer), circuits open in tabs (the dock's document panes), Back and Forward through the places visited,
and Go To (CGoToDialog).  Items 3 to 6 of the list of what to bring over from the IDE (1 dock panes, 2 Settings'
Keymap, are done).


Decided (user, 2026-11-14)
--------------------------

- The Simulation Tree replaces the placeholder Simulations pane: a dockable tree of the simulated circuit's
  instances, nested; activating a node enters that instance.  View > Simulation Tree shows it.
- The Subcircuits pane is a tree of what uses what: at the top the circuits nothing else uses (Main), under each
  circuit the distinct circuits it uses, so one used in several places appears under each.  Choosing one edits it,
  as the list does now.
- A tab is a place: a top circuit, the instance path into it and the zoom and centre.  There is one editor, one
  simulation and one undo history: the active tab moves the editor there.  A tab inside the circuit simulated keeps
  its simulation; a tab on another top circuit restarts the simulation on it, as choosing it in Subcircuits does.
- The open tabs are saved in the project file.
- Go To finds each instance in the simulation: U12 inside both Audio Boards is two results, and choosing one enters
  that instance path and selects it, its live state showing.  Components are found by type ("74HC00") as well as by
  name (2026-11-14).
- Back and Forward step through moves between places (entering or leaving a subcircuit, choosing a circuit, switching
  tab, a tree's node, Go To), each place coming back with the zoom and centre it had.  Zooming and panning aren't
  steps.  Keys Ctrl+Alt+Left and Ctrl+Alt+Right, as the IDE's.


Design
------

A place (SLogicimPlace): the top circuit's type name, the instance path into it as the editor writes it
(CCircuitEditor::GetInstancePathText, "12/3", each instance's index in the circuit before it), the zoom and the
centre.  CLogicimWindowContent::ShowPlace(place) is the one way to move the editor: Simulate the top circuit if it
isn't the one simulated, else LeaveInstances; enter each instance on the path (CCircuitEditor::FindInstancePath);
set the viewport.  It fails, changing nothing, for a place that's gone (a circuit removed, a path whose instance
isn't there after an edit or an undo); Back, Forward and the saved tabs skip such places.

The editor is the only thing that edits.  Each tab has its own canvas and canvas draw (its own viewport); the active
tab's canvas is the editor's.  A canvas that's visible but not active (the dock split in two) draws its own place:
CCircuitCanvasDraw paints a given circuit and view path, with the simulation's state if its place is in the circuit
simulated, without it otherwise.

Undo restores the editor's circuit and path with the snapshot: the active tab follows it there (its place changes),
and that's a step for Back.  Tabs aren't in undo snapshots: loading a snapshot leaves them as they are.

The trees are made again only when what they show changed (as ListSubcircuits does): the circuits, or the simulated
circuit's instances.  A big import has thousands of instances, so the Simulation tree's nodes below the top are
made as they are opened if making them all is too slow (CTreeContainer has no lazy nodes yet: added if needed).


Plan
----

Each numbered step is built, checked and committed on its own (LogiCim on main, CommonLibraries on master).  Checks
are LogiCim's (LogicimChecks.py) with new check steps, TestWindowLib for anything in WindowLib, and TestLogicimLib
for anything in LogicimLib; the real panes are tried on the isolated X server.

1. Places
   1.1 SLogicimPlace, and CLogicimWindowContent::GetPlace and ShowPlace as above; EnterSubcircuit, LeaveSubcircuit
       and SubcircuitSelected go through ShowPlace.
   1.2 CCircuitEditor (LogicimLib) can enter a path from its top in one call, if FindInstancePath and EnterInstance
       don't already make it one; TestLogicimLib.
   1.3 Check steps: place (prints the current place), showplace:<top>:<path>.  Checks: a nested project's places,
       a gone place refused.

2. The Simulation tree
   2.1 The Simulations pane becomes the Simulation pane: a CTreeContainer, its top the circuit simulated, under each
       circuit its subcircuit instances in the circuit's order, each "U3 (Audio Board)", its node's data the
       instance's index.  The pane's ID changes; a saved layout that has the old one lays the new one out as its
       default.
   2.2 It follows the editor: the current place's node selected and its parents opened.
   2.3 Activating a node (a double click, or enter) shows its place; a click only selects.
   2.4 View > Simulation Tree shows and activates the pane (View.SimulationTree runs, no longer logged); View >
       Simulations goes.  New Simulation... is left as it is.
   2.5 Check step simtree (prints the tree, the selected node marked); checks on a nested project.
   2.6 Commit.

3. The Subcircuits tree
   3.1 The Subcircuits pane becomes a CTreeContainer: the circuits nothing uses at the top, under each the distinct
       circuits it uses, sorted as the project lists them.
   3.2 Choosing a node edits that circuit, as now; the edited circuit's first node selected.
   3.3 Check step subcircuits (prints the tree); checks; commit.

4. Back and Forward
   4.1 CLogicimNavigationHistory: places back and forward (at most 50), a move recording the place left (with its
       zoom and centre as they were) and clearing forward.  The IDE's CIDENavigationHistory is about files and lines,
       so LogiCim's is its own.
   4.2 Commands Navigate.Back and Navigate.Forward (Ctrl+Alt+Left, Ctrl+Alt+Right), in the View menu and the toolbar,
       off when there's nothing to go to.
   4.3 Places that are gone are skipped.
   4.4 Check steps back, forward and history; checks; commit.

5. Tabs
   5.1 Each tab a dock document pane holding its own CCircuitCanvas and draw; the "Circuit" pane is the first.  The
       active pane's place is the editor's (CDockManagerListener::ActivePaneChanged shows it); its title is the
       place's circuit, with its instance ("Counter in U3").
   5.2 Open in New Tab: on a subcircuit instance's canvas menu, and on the Simulation and Subcircuits trees' nodes
       (a menu, or a middle click).  Otherwise moves happen in the active tab.  Tabs close, except the last.
   5.3 A visible tab that isn't active draws its own place (CCircuitCanvasDraw given a circuit and view path).
   5.4 Undo moving the editor moves the active tab's place.
   5.5 Saved in the project: <Editor> holds a <Tab> per tab (top circuit, path, zoom, centre) and which is active;
       written by Save, read by Load, not by undo.  A tab doesn't make a change to save: it's written next time.
       TestLogicimLib for the Editor element (LogicimLib).
   5.6 Check steps tabs, newtab, activatetab:<n>, closetab:<n>; checks; commit.

6. Go To
   6.1 Navigate.GoTo (Ctrl+N), a CGoToDialog with tabs: Components (every component instance in the simulation by
       name or by type, "U12 74HC00" with "Main > U3 > U1" after it, the part matched highlighted; a subcircuit
       instance's type is its circuit's), Nets (tunnels and labels by name, each instance) and Subcircuits
       (circuits by type name, edited at their top).
   6.2 Its searcher walks the simulated circuit's instances, as the Simulation tree does; CNameMatcher matches.
   6.3 Choosing a result shows its place (a step for Back), selects the component and centres on it.
   6.4 Check step goto:<tab>:<text> (prints the results) and gotopick:<n>; checks; commit.

7. Finish
   7.1 This note's status; every check and test passing.


Where it stands (2026-11-14)
----------------------------

Steps 1 to 7 are built, checked and committed.  LogiCim's checks have one for each step (places, simulation-tree,
subcircuit-tree, back-forward, tabs, go-to) on Checks/Nested.xml; the panes, tabs, menus and Go To were also tried
by hand on the isolated X server.  What was built differs from the plan in these ways:

- A place is LogicimLib's (SCircuitPlace in CircuitPlace.h), not LogiCim's, so the editor can keep the tabs with the
  project (CCircuitEditor::SetTabs, <Tab> elements in <Editor>, left alone by undo).  The editor enters a path in one
  step (EnterInstancePath) and finds a path's view path in the simulation (GetInstanceViewPath).
- The commands are View's, as LogiCim has no Navigate menu: View.Back, View.Forward and View.GoTo (Go To..., Ctrl+N),
  in the View menu.  Back and Forward have no toolbar buttons: LogiCim's toolbar icons are KiCad's and have no arrows.
- The first tab (the Circuit pane) is the one that can't be closed, rather than whichever is last: a tab's close
  cross is made with its tab.  Window, New Tab opens the place edited in a tab of its own.
- Open in New Tab is on the canvas menu of a subcircuit instance and on both trees' menus; there's no middle click.
  A right click on the Subcircuits tree selects the node under it, as its list did, which edits that circuit.
- Go To finds components and nets in the circuit simulated and its instances; other circuits are found on its
  Subcircuits tab.  A component's part name is CComponentView::AppendPartName.
- The Simulation tree makes all its nodes at once: on Audio Board (27 instances, 2204 components) the trees and Go To
  take no time to notice, so it needs no lazy nodes.
- The status bar's zoom follows a canvas fitted as it's first drawn (a new tab's), at the next tick.

Places that change (2026-11-14)
-------------------------------

- A path names each instance as well as its index ("1:U2/1:U3", InstancePath.h), and finds it by name, so a place
  still finds its instance after other components in its circuit come and go; by index between two of a name, or in
  a path written before names ("1/1").
- The editor tells a listener (CCircuitEditorListener::ProjectChanged) after each change it saves for undo and after
  each undo or redo; LogiCim's content then updates its panes, tabs and status.  So the trees follow edits too.
- Each tab that isn't edited then checks its place: its path rewritten as it is now; trimmed to as far down it as is
  left (fitted when it's next edited) if an instance on it went; closed if its circuit went, the first tab instead
  going to the top of the circuit simulated.  Back and Forward still skip places that are gone.

Nothing is left open.
